01 / THE IDEA

Functions to optimize.
Agents to evaluate.

For the Solver, evaluation means querying a function at the next chosen point. For the Bench, it means testing an agent's ability to make those choices. Black-box optimization becomes a test of sequential decision-making under a finite evaluation budget.

03 / THE BOUNDARY

A

Opaque by default

The solver receives an objective, not its identity, implementation, or known optimum.

B

Budget is the clock

Progress is measured in real function evaluations, not wall-clock speed.

C

Evidence stays attached

Profiles are derived views; source-local histories and provenance remain inspectable.
